# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (not yet released)

First public release.

- Capture selected text or a screen region from any app with ⌃⌥Space, with its source: page address, file and PDF page, or app and window.
- A note with `@person` and `#tag`, and what the clip is for: Save, Revise, Call or To-do.
- Clips saved as markdown files, with screenshots beside them, in `~/Clipback/Personal` and `~/Clipback/Work`.
- The review window: Today (spaced revision), Inbox, Revise, Calls (an agenda per person), To-do, Sources and Archive, with search.
- Work and Personal libraries, picked by work hours and by apps you list in Settings.
- Text in screenshots read on your Mac, searchable and selectable.
- Hidden from screen shares. No network access.
